What it does

Less admin. One tap where it counts.

Ask for things the way you'd ask a person. It does them, then tells you in one line. The only time it waits for you is when something is about to leave your account.

Ask, don't tap around

"Move gym to 8." "What's on this week?" "Book 30 minutes with Ayşe on Friday." It checks your calendar first, does it, and confirms in one line.

Replies you approve

It reads the thread and writes the reply you'd write. The draft appears in the chat with Send, Keep and Delete. Nothing leaves without your tap.

A calendar that looks before it leaps

Adds, moves and clears events. Keeps the length when you only move the start. Tells you before it books over something else.

A to-do list you both keep

"I need to renew my Oyster card" lands on the list. "Done with the report" ticks it off. Tick things yourself in the To-do tab and it stays in sync. It offers to add deadlines it spots in your email.

Reminders and a morning brief

"Remind me at 6 to call mum." Every morning: today's schedule, emails that need you, and what's due this week, in a message you can read in ten seconds.

It remembers you

Tell it once that you train Mon, Wed and Fri, or that Kerem is your flatmate, and it just knows. Everything it remembers is listed in the Memory tab, and you can remove any of it.

Talk or type

Hold the mic and say it, in English or Turkish. It shows what it heard, then acts. Send a photo of a timetable or a poster and it pulls out the dates.

Watches your inbox so you don't have to

It checks new mail through the day and pings you only when something matters: a real person, an interview, a deadline. Newsletters and receipts stay quiet.

Privacy and control

It works for you. Only you.

An assistant that reads your email has to earn that. These are the rules it lives by, written so you can hold us to them.

Nothing is sent without you

Email goes out only when you tap Send on a draft. There is no setting that changes this.

You choose the access

Google asks you exactly what to share. Grant Gmail, Calendar or both, and revoke it from your Google account whenever you like.

See everything it knows

Every fact it remembers is listed in the Memory tab. Remove one and it's forgotten for good.

Delete everything, any time

One button removes your account, your history, your memories and your to-dos. Not hidden, not delayed.

Your data is not the product

We don't sell it, we don't show you ads, and we don't use your email to train anything.

It ignores everyone else

On WhatsApp and Telegram it answers you and no one else. Messages from other people never reach it.

The app

Four tabs. Nothing you don't need.

Chat, To-do, Memory and Settings. Light and dark. Hold to talk. Built for iPhone and Android.

FAQ

Questions people ask first.

When can I use it?

We're letting people in from the waitlist in small batches so every account gets attention. Join above and we'll email you when it's your turn.

Can it send email on its own?

No. It reads and searches your email and writes drafts. Every draft comes to you with Send, Keep and Delete. Sending always needs your tap.

What does it need access to?

Signing in needs only your email address (Google, Apple or a code we send you). Gmail and Google Calendar are a separate step you grant through Google's own consent screen, and you can revoke that later from your Google account settings.

Which phones does it work on?

iPhone and Android. You can also talk to it on WhatsApp or Telegram, and it's the same assistant with the same memory wherever you message from.

What does it cost?

Pricing is announced when we open up. Waitlist members hear first and get the launch offer.

How do I delete my data?

Settings has a delete button that removes your account, history, memories and to-dos. You can also forget individual memories from the Memory tab at any time.

Which languages does it understand?

English and Turkish are fully supported for both typing and voice notes. Most other major languages work too.
