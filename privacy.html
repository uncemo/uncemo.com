Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 October 2026 · Applies to the Uncemo app and uncemo.com

Uncemo is a personal assistant that, with your permission, reads your calendar and email so it can act on your behalf. That only works if you can trust what we do with your data. This page says exactly what we collect, what we do with it, and how to make us delete it. It is written to be read, not skimmed.

Who we are

Uncemo ("we", "us") operates this service. You can reach us at privacy@uncemo.com.

Google and Microsoft data

Connecting Google or Microsoft is optional and separate from signing in. When you connect, the provider asks you to grant these permissions, and you can revoke them at any time from the Account tab in the app, from your Google account's third-party access page, or from your Microsoft account's privacy settings.

  • Calendar (Google Calendar, Outlook calendar). Used to read your events and to create, move or delete events when you ask.
  • Mail (Gmail, Outlook). Gmail is a separate add-on you can decline: Google Calendar works without it. Used to search and read messages you ask about, to watch your inbox for messages that need a reply, to create and update drafts in your Drafts folder, and to archive a message when you ask. The assistant never sends email: you open the draft in Gmail or Outlook and send it yourself. It never deletes email.

Email and calendar content is read when a request needs it and is passed to our AI provider to produce the answer (see "Who else sees your data"). We keep the ids of inbox messages we have already checked so we don't alert you twice. We do not store copies of your email. Your access tokens are stored encrypted on our server.

Uncemo's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In plain terms: Google data is used only to provide the features you see in the app, is never used for advertising, is never sold, and is never used to train AI models. The same rules apply to Microsoft data.

Your phone's calendars and contacts

Two switches in Account, both off until you turn them on. "Calendars on this phone" sends the next two weeks of events from the calendars on your phone (title, time, place and calendar name) so the assistant sees them next to Google and Outlook; it can read them but not change them. "Contacts" sends names, email addresses and phone numbers so that "email Ada" or "text Jake" finds the right person; nothing else from a contact is sent. The phone refreshes both while the switch is on. Turning a switch off deletes our copy at once.

Who else sees your data

  • Anthropic (the Claude API) receives the content of your request, relevant email or calendar text, your memories and to-dos, so it can generate the assistant's reply. Anthropic does not use API data to train its models.
  • OpenAI receives the audio of voice notes to transcribe them. Nothing else is sent.
  • Google and Microsoft receive the requests needed to read and change your calendar and mail when you connect those accounts.
  • Supabase handles sign-in and stores your email address and sign-in method.
  • Railway hosts our server and database.
  • Expo delivers push notifications. The notification text passes through their service.
  • Resend sends sign-in codes and waitlist emails.
  • If paid plans launch and you buy one, Apple or Google take the payment and RevenueCat processes the receipt. We never see your card details.

We never sell your data, never share it with advertisers, and never show you ads.

Where your data lives

Each account has its own separate database file on our server. There is no shared table of everyone's messages. Access tokens from Google and Microsoft are encrypted at rest.

How long we keep it

For as long as you have an account. Daily usage counts are kept for 90 days. Phone calendar and contact copies are deleted the moment you turn the switch off. Waitlist emails are kept until we launch or you ask us to remove them.

Deleting your data

Account tab, then "Delete my account and data". This immediately removes your account, your Google and Microsoft tokens, your conversation history, memories, to-dos, reminders, phone calendar and contact copies, feedback, push tokens and usage counts, and deletes your sign-in record at Supabase. We also revoke our access at Google. It cannot be undone. You can also email privacy@uncemo.com and we will do it for you within 7 days.

You can forget individual memories from the Memory tab at any time, and disconnect Google or Microsoft without deleting your account.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Access tokens are encrypted on disk. Sign-in uses one-time codes or Google and Apple identity; we never see or store a password. Our server only ever acts on a request carrying your signed session token.

Your rights

If you are in the UK or EU you have the right to access, correct, export and delete your data, and to object to or restrict processing. Email us and we will respond within 30 days. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office.

Children

Uncemo is not for people under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect. The date at the top always shows the current version.
